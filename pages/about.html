Stories are the lasting mark we leave on this planet and through photos and film we can share them in a way that will transcend generations.

I offer photography and videography services in Golden, British Columbia and surrounding areas.

Ever since I was young, I’ve been interested in documenting my life. As an athlete and artist most of my work reflects natural landscapes and the sports within them.

A perk of the job is meeting and working with amazing individuals and the inside look at these remarkable people through her work.

If you have a story you want told or an event captured please reach out as I’d love to work together.
