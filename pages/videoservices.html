Video Production Services



Events

Starting at $850

For individuals, and organizations looking for a cinematic documentary style film of their event. Anything from community gatherings and performances. This package offers candid documentation of the events unfolding.



All event packages include:

  • Pre-shoot planning to customize offerings to your event and the elements you want to capture.
  • Colour grading and sound design
  • Professional filming and editing
  • Cinematic highlight film

Packages may be customized based on:

  • Hours of coverage
  • Audio requirements (speeches ect.)
  • Number of videographers required
  • Location(s)
  • Turn around time
  • Film length

Commercial

Starting at $990

For brands, creatives, and small businesses looking for cinematic video content to elevate their online presence and marketing. These videos are designed to tell your story, showcase your product or service, and create engaging content for web and social platforms.



All commercial packages include:

  • Pre-production planning session (concept, goals, shot list)
  • Creative direction during filming.
  • Professional filming and editing
  • Cinematic brand video (duration discussed upon inquiry)
  • Colour grading and sound design
  • Licensed music

Packages may be customized based on:

  • Shoot duration
  • Number of filming locations
  • Audio capture (interviews, dialogue)
  • Scripted vs. documentary style
  • Number of final videos and revisions (eg. short form and long form content)
  • Travel

Add-ons:

  • Social media cutdowns (vertical reels)
  • Multiple videos from one shoot
  • Rush Delivery

Every brand has different needs, so commercial packages are customized to fit your vision, timeline, and usage.
